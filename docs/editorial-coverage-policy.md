# Editorial Coverage Policy

## Mission

SouthernVT curates memorable Southern Vermont experiences through trusted editorial recommendations. We prioritize editorial usefulness and quality over listing every possible location.

## Coverage philosophy

### Core Southern Vermont

Primary editorial focus:

- Windham County
- Bennington County
- Southern Windsor County

### Worth the Drive

Exceptional destinations outside core coverage that naturally improve Southern Vermont trip planning. These are clearly labeled.

### Regional Feature

Cross-boundary editorial packages where topic relevance matters most, including:

- Road trips
- Fall foliage
- Scenic drives
- Breweries
- Covered bridge routes

## Editorial standards

SouthernVT is guided by:

- Original storytelling
- Editorial independence
- Verification
- Photography
- Community relevance

Businesses cannot buy:

- Recommendations
- Verification
- Editorial rankings
- SouthernVT Recommended status

## Badge meanings

- 📍 **Core Southern Vermont**: Primary editorial geography.
- 🚗 **Worth the Drive**: Near-core destinations that strengthen reader itineraries.
- 🗺 **Regional Feature**: Cross-regional thematic coverage.
