# Compass Roadmap

## 1) Compass Core

- Stabilize shared platform modules (auth, analytics, graph, workflow, search, AI, notifications).
- Keep publication-specific branding and vocabulary out of core modules.
- Expand publication configuration contracts for theming, nav, and editorial surfaces.

## 2) SouthernVT

- Preserve travel publication behavior as a Compass publication profile.
- Continue compatibility for existing place-first public routes and editorial data.

## 3) MadeInVT

- Continue maker-first publication modeling on top of Compass Core.
- Keep maker vocabulary and public branding in publication config and content layers.

## 4) Future publications

- Add new publication profiles (for example White Mountains Guide, Cape Cod Explorer, Berkshire Made, Adirondack Guide).
- Reuse Compass Core with publication-specific branding, navigation, vocabulary, and editorial framing.
