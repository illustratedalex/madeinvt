# SouthernVT Editorial Standards

## Mission

**SouthernVT exists to become the most trusted guide to Southern Vermont.**

We are not a marketplace. We are not a booking engine. We are not a review aggregator.

We are a carefully curated editorial experience where every destination has been personally visited, verified, and described with the kind of specific, useful detail that makes a traveler feel like they're getting insider knowledge.

### Core Values

- **Accuracy**: Every fact is checked. Every detail is current. Nothing is guessed or exaggerated.
- **Originality**: We write our own stories. We take our own photos. We don't syndicate or repurpose.
- **Photography**: Visual storytelling that shows why a place matters, not just that it exists.
- **Trust**: Our reputation is built on consistency, honesty, and refusing to compromise editorial integrity for revenue.
- **Verification**: Badges are earned through actual work—visiting, photographing, and validating—not through payment or partnership.
- **Storytelling**: We write like travel writers, not marketing departments. Every destination has a human story.

---

## Every Flagship Place Must Include

### Content Components

1. **Editorial Story** — A narrative piece (3-5 minutes reading time) that captures the character, seasonality, and why locals return
2. **Why Visit** — Visual cards highlighting 3-5 compelling reasons specific to this destination
3. **Quick Facts** — Essential planning information (distance, difficulty, parking, best season, etc.)
4. **Visitor Tips** — 5-7 actionable recommendations for safe, respectful, enjoyable visits
5. **Photography Tips** — Technical and compositional guidance for capturing the place well
6. **Nearby Adventures** — 4-6 complementary destinations within 20-30 minutes
7. **Nearby Food** — At least 2-3 restaurants, cafes, or food establishments
8. **Nearby Lodging** — At least 2-3 accommodation options (inns, hotels, campgrounds)
9. **Collections** — Featured placement in at least 3 SouthernVT curated collections
10. **Suggested Day** — A complete itinerary example (breakfast → place → nearby activity → dinner)
11. **Verification** — Badges indicating editorial status and travel validation
12. **SEO** — Optimized title, description, and metadata for discovery

## Editorial Voice

### We Write Like

- **National Geographic Traveler** — Specific, beautiful language with strong visual imagery
- **Outside Magazine** — Knowledgeable, practical advice rooted in real experience
- **Yankee Magazine** — Regional character, local context, and seasonal wisdom

### We Are

- **Friendly** — Approachable, warm, conversational without being casual
- **Knowledgeable** — Deep enough to be useful, never condescending
- **Honest** — We name limitations. We acknowledge crowds or difficulty. We warn about hazards.
- **Specific** — "Scenic," "fun," and "beautiful" are forbidden. Show, don't summarize.

### We Never Are

- **Salesy** — We don't use marketing language. No "must-see," "don't miss," "hidden gem" hype.
- **Exaggerated** — Superlatives are earned, not claimed. "The best" only if truly documented.
- **AI-generated** — Every word is written by a human. We don't use AI content, summaries, or descriptions.
- **Repetitive** — Each destination has its own voice and story, never templated boilerplate.

### Writing Standards

**Story Structure**
- Open with a scene or moment, not a summary
- Ground the reader in geography and season
- Explain why the place matters to people who already know the region
- Close with actionable insight or emotional resonance
- 3-5 minute reading time (approximately 600-800 words)

**Tone Examples**
- ✅ "The river corridor here moves fast enough to be interesting, slow enough for swimming."
- ❌ "A stunning waterway perfect for adventure."
- ✅ "Fall colors peak mid-October when the surrounding maples turn from yellow to deep red."
- ❌ "Beautiful autumn foliage season."
- ✅ "Bring water shoes—river stones are smooth but require grip, especially after rain."
- ❌ "Recommended footwear for comfort."

## Verification Standards

### Verification Badges

Badges are **never purchased**. They are **earned through actual work**.

#### Location Verified
- Physical visit to the address/location confirmed
- Map coordinates validated with GPS
- Boundaries and access points documented
- Current as of the publication date

#### Photo Verified
- All featured images were photographed by SouthernVT
- Images represent actual conditions (not stock photos or archives)
- Gallery reflects seasonal reality and typical visitor experience
- Drone/aerial imagery clearly labeled where used

#### Personally Visited
- Destination visited by editorial team member
- Visit was recent enough for current conditions (within 6-12 months)
- Firsthand impressions inform the story
- Visitor tips are from actual experience, not guidance documents

#### SouthernVT Recommended
- Meets flagship editorial standards (95%+ content health)
- Demonstrates consistent visitor value across seasons
- Represents the quality benchmark for future destinations
- Receives editorial feature placement and promotion

### Verification Ethics

- **Verification cannot be bought.** Partner status, sponsorship, or payment never grants badges.
- **Verification is not about being famous.** Recommended status is about editorial quality and trustworthiness, not visitor volume.
- **Verification is maintained, not permanent.** If a place changes hands, closes seasonally, or degrades, verification is reviewed and updated.
- **Verification is public.** We show what we've verified and how recent the verification is.

## Photography Standards

### Required Components

**Hero Image**
- Large, evocative photograph representing the destination's character
- Minimum 1200px width, high resolution (300dpi for print)
- Natural lighting preferred (not overly processed or filtered)
- Shows the place, not just a pretty landscape
- Taken on-site by SouthernVT or licensed from verified photographer

**Gallery**
- Minimum 4 images showing different aspects of the destination
- Should include: arrival view, main activity, close detail, seasonal variation
- Mix of landscape and portrait orientation (for social media adaptation)
- Consistent visual style and color palette
- All images verified as recent and accurate

### Optional Enhancements

**Drone Photography**
- Aerial perspective showing scale, layout, or setting context
- Licensed drone footage or taken with proper permits
- Clearly labeled "Drone" to set expectations
- Shows approach and access, not just pretty altitude shots

**Vertical Reel / Short-Form Video**
- 15-30 second visual summary for Instagram/TikTok sharing
- Shows movement, sound, and dynamic experience
- Authentic footage, no heavy editing or music
- Optional—not required for launch

**360-Degree Photography**
- Immersive virtual tour of key location points
- Tools: Matterport, Google Street View, or equivalent
- Optional—typically used for complex indoor spaces or landmarks
- Not required for most outdoor destinations

## Content Health Scoring

Content Health is calculated across story quality, photography, metadata, completeness, and engagement readiness.

### Minimum Launch Standard: 80%

**Required for Public Publication**
- Editorial story complete (3+ minutes)
- Hero image and 3+ gallery images
- Quick facts covering basics (distance, difficulty, season, parking)
- Visitor tips (3+ items)
- Photography tips (2+ items)
- SEO metadata (title, description, keywords)
- At least 1 verification badge

**Not yet flagship, but ready for discovery**

### Flagship Standard: 95%

**Benchmark for Excellence**
- Editorial story (5 minutes, full seasonal context)
- Hero image + 4+ gallery images (including seasonal variation)
- Comprehensive quick facts (8+ items including accessibility, cell service, etc.)
- Full visitor tips (5-7 items with specific actionable advice)
- Complete photography tips (4+ items with technical guidance)
- 4+ nearby adventures (with descriptions)
- 2+ nearby food options and 2+ nearby lodging options
- Featured in 3+ SouthernVT collections
- Complete suggested day itinerary
- 3+ verification badges with recent visit confirmation
- SEO optimized for discovery and local search
- Smooth page performance and accessibility

**These destinations represent the future standard for SouthernVT**

## Place Checklist for Flagship Status

Use this checklist when developing any new flagship destination or auditing existing content.

### Story & Voice
- [ ] Editorial story written (600-800 words, 4-5 min read)
- [ ] Story captures character and seasonality
- [ ] Story explains why locals return
- [ ] Story avoids marketing language and exaggeration
- [ ] Story includes specific details (times, months, distances, hazards)
- [ ] Story is written in SouthernVT voice (travel magazine, not marketing)

### Photography
- [ ] Hero image selected (1200px+, high quality)
- [ ] Gallery has 4+ images showing different aspects
- [ ] Images include seasonal variation where relevant
- [ ] All images are original SouthernVT photography
- [ ] Optional: Drone image labeled appropriately
- [ ] Optional: 360 tour or vertical video created

### Quick Facts
- [ ] Best season documented
- [ ] Visit length estimated (hours or duration)
- [ ] Distance or location details included
- [ ] Difficulty level noted (if applicable)
- [ ] Parking information provided
- [ ] Restroom availability noted
- [ ] Dog-friendly status clarified
- [ ] Cell service availability included
- [ ] Accessibility information (if applicable)
- [ ] At least 8-10 facts minimum

### Visitor Tips
- [ ] 5-7 actionable tips provided
- [ ] Tips include safety information
- [ ] Tips include best times/strategies
- [ ] Tips avoid obvious statements
- [ ] Tips are based on actual experience
- [ ] Each tip is specific (not "wear comfortable shoes")

### Photography Tips
- [ ] 4-6 tips for capturing the place well
- [ ] Tips include technical guidance (focal length, time of day)
- [ ] Tips address seasonal photo opportunities
- [ ] Tips include composition guidance
- [ ] Drone tip placeholder noted if relevant
- [ ] Tips assume modest photography knowledge

### Collections & Discovery
- [ ] Place appears in 3+ SouthernVT collections
- [ ] Collections are thematically relevant
- [ ] Search keywords optimized for discovery
- [ ] Related places clearly linked
- [ ] Compass Engine compatibility verified

### Nearby Adventures, Food & Lodging
- [ ] 4-6 nearby adventures identified (with descriptions)
- [ ] At least 2-3 nearby food options included
- [ ] At least 2-3 nearby lodging options included
- [ ] All nearby places are linked and discoverable
- [ ] Distances and drive times noted

### Suggested Day
- [ ] Complete itinerary structure provided
- [ ] Breakfast location suggested
- [ ] Main activity placed strategically
- [ ] Secondary activity or location included
- [ ] Meal or break point included
- [ ] Dinner destination suggested
- [ ] Timeline is realistic (not overpacked)

### SEO & Metadata
- [ ] SEO title written (60 chars, includes keywords)
- [ ] SEO description written (160 chars, benefit-focused)
- [ ] OpenGraph image optimized
- [ ] Twitter card optimized
- [ ] Schema markup validates
- [ ] Meta keywords include seasonal and regional terms
- [ ] Page title matches editorial quality

### Verification
- [ ] Location Verified badge earned
- [ ] Photo Verified badge earned
- [ ] Personally Visited badge earned (if flagship)
- [ ] SouthernVT Recommended badge earned (if flagship)
- [ ] Verification dates current (within 6-12 months)
- [ ] Verification reason documented

### Quality Assurance
- [ ] Content health score: 95% (flagship)
- [ ] Discovery score: 90%+ (high visibility in recommendations)
- [ ] Accessibility audit: WCAG AA compliant
- [ ] Mobile experience: responsive and fast
- [ ] All links tested and working
- [ ] No typos or grammar errors
- [ ] Copy edited by second reviewer
- [ ] Final approval from editorial lead

## Governance & Updates

### Review Schedule

- **Quarterly**: Review flagship destinations for accuracy and current information
- **Seasonally**: Update photography, seasonal tips, best-time-to-visit guidance
- **Annually**: Conduct full editorial refresh on each destination
- **As-needed**: Emergency updates for closures, major changes, or hazards

### Editorial Authority

The editorial team maintains final authority on:
- Voice consistency across all destinations
- Verification badge eligibility
- Content health scoring methodology
- Feature prioritization and homepage placement
- Seasonal promotion and collection assignment

### Expansion Criteria

New destinations are considered for flagship status when they:
- Have received multiple visitor inquiries or comments
- Fill a geographic or category gap in current coverage
- Demonstrate sustained visitor interest and engagement
- Have clear photography and access opportunities
- Represent authentic Southern Vermont culture and experience
