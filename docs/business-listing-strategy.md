# Business Listing Strategy

SouthernVT needs a basic public business directory so local businesses can be discovered before every profile is fully built out. Basic listings create coverage first, then improve over time through owner claims, editorial review, and verification.

## Why basic listings exist

- They give travelers a broader, more useful directory foundation.
- They let SouthernVT seed discovery coverage in towns that do not yet have full editorial profiles.
- They create a clear upgrade path from incomplete listing to accurate owner-maintained record.

## How claiming works

- SouthernVT can seed a basic listing from public or submitted information.
- A business owner can use the public claim flow to request ownership.
- Claimed listings can be improved over time with better contact details, description updates, and future partner tools.
- Claiming does not guarantee verification or special placement.

## Status definitions

- Basic: Created by SouthernVT from publicly available or submitted information. May be incomplete.
- Claimed: Business owner has claimed the profile.
- Verified: SouthernVT has reviewed or verified key details.
- Founding Partner: Business is helping support SouthernVT during beta.
- Premium: Reserved for future product planning and not used as part of this mock foundation.

## Editorial independence

- Basic listings are not paid sponsorships.
- Founding Partner support does not control rankings, editorial coverage, or verification decisions.
- SouthernVT should keep a clean separation between directory operations and editorial judgment.

## Verification policy

- Verification cannot be bought.
- Verification means SouthernVT reviewed or confirmed key details.
- Unverified listings should avoid overclaiming and should clearly signal that details may be incomplete.
