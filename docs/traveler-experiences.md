# Traveler Experiences

SouthernVT now includes a compact **Traveler Experiences** section at the bottom of listing pages.

## Purpose

This is not a public review platform with endless threads. It is a curated editorial publication that includes short, verified visitor signals.

## Difference between editorial and traveler signals

### Editorial Review

Editorial Review is SouthernVT's internal and field-based confidence layer. It can include:

- Personally Visited
- Photo Verified
- SouthernVT Recommended

These signals come from editorial and verification workflows.

### Traveler Experiences

Traveler Experiences are short, magazine-style notes from verified visitors:

- Overall experience summary
- Small category ratings by listing type
- 3-5 concise traveler notes
- Optional business response

The section is intentionally limited in size and scope.

### Verification

Verification indicates SouthernVT confidence in core factual and on-the-ground details. Verification is separate from business payments and separate from crowd ranking systems.
