# Content Quality and Completeness

## Completeness Score

Places now use a weighted completeness score to show what content is ready and what still needs work.

The score is calculated from 12 checks that total 100 points:

- Name: 5
- Description: 10
- Place Type: 5
- Address or coordinates: 10
- Featured Image: 15
- Gallery with at least 3 images: 15
- Categories: 5
- Tags: 5
- Amenities: 5
- Hours/contact/website: 5
- Related Places: 10
- SEO title/description: 10

## Launch-Ready Criteria

A Place is considered launch-ready when it reaches at least 80% completeness.

This threshold is used in Basecamp readiness reporting to highlight content that can ship with lower editorial risk.

## Recommended Minimum

Recommended minimum completeness for Place publishing:

- 80% or higher

Below 80%, focus first on missing photos, relationships, and SEO fields because those have the largest effect on usability and discoverability.
