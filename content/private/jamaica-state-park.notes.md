# Research

- Vermont State Parks operates Jamaica State Park. Primary source: vtstateparks.com/jamaica.
- The West River Railroad grade history: the Williamsville to South Londonderry line operated 1880–1936. The trail follows the former rail bed — this historical layer adds editorial depth.
- The West River flood of 1927 (the "Great Flood") significantly altered the river channel. The park landscape was shaped by that event — worth a sentence in the story.
- Spring ball drop: the annual release of a numbered ball upstream during the canoe race. Research the exact event name, typical date (early April), and whether public spectating is accessible.
- Research current campground reservation system — Vermont State Parks moved to a new platform. Confirm current URL and booking lead time.

# Seasonal Notes

- **April (ball drop):** Whitewater race energy, high water, crowds on race day. Campground not yet open.
- **Mid-May through June:** Campground open, river still moderate flow. Good early-season content.
- **July–August:** Peak season. Swim area active, campground full weekends. Arrive before 9 a.m. for day use.
- **September–October:** Campground thins after Labor Day. Rail trail foliage. Quieter, more editorial atmosphere.
- **Winter:** Campground closed, trails open. Snowshoe content possible but limited audience.

# Local Tips

- The lean-to sites closest to the river fill first — if you want river-proximate camping, book the moment reservations open (typically early March for peak summer dates).
- The Wednesday after Labor Day is one of the best days to visit — campground dramatically quieter, weather still warm, trail in excellent condition.
- The ranger staff at Jamaica State Park are unusually knowledgeable about the local area. Worth a conversation for editorial tips.
- The West River swimming is best in the stretch between the day-use area and the first bend — avoid the deeper pools near the railroad bridge abutments where currents are unpredictable.
