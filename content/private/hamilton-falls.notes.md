# Seasonal Notes

- **April–May:** Mud season. Trail extremely muddy, potential high water. Do not shoot the swimming pool — it is dangerous and the image sends wrong signal.
- **Late May–June:** Peak flow, green walls, mist at the base. Best dramatic photography window.
- **July–August:** Calm water, swimming context possible. Best visitor guidance photography.
- **September–October:** Fall color on approach trail. Lower water. Best foliage shots from the ledge.
- **November–March:** Closed to all photography missions — icy, no facilities, and no audience for winter content here.

# Local Tips

- The falls are at their absolute best 48–72 hours after a significant rain. Flow is high but the trail hasn't become dangerously slick yet.
- Local Vermont families use the upper ledge area on the far side of the falls for a quieter perch away from the main viewing area. Worth noting in the public story eventually.
- The drive in on Cobb Brook Road is unpaved and rough in mud season — note for visitors with low-clearance vehicles.
- Jamaica village has a small general store that opens early enough for pre-hike coffee. Worth including in the Nearby Food section.
