The Vermont Country Store in Weston is one of the most visited retail destinations in Vermont — a working general store with roots in 1946, famous for its catalog business and its floor-to-ceiling shelves of practical and nostalgic goods. The Orton family has operated it for three generations.

Notes for writer: The store is genuinely worth visiting, not just a tourist trap. The range of products is unusual — think useful Vermont pantry staples alongside retro candy and hard-to-find housewares. The Weston village setting (covered bridge, village green, Weston Priory nearby) elevates the stop into a half-day destination rather than a quick shop. There's also a Rockingham location but Weston is the flagship.

**Fall (September–October):** Peak traffic but worth it — the village and store are at their most atmospheric.

**Holiday season (November–December):** Exceptionally well-merchandised for Christmas gifts and Vermont pantry items.

**Summer:** Less crowded than fall. Good anchor for a Route 100 driving day.
