Hogback Mountain in Marlboro is best known for its roadside overlook on Route 9 (the Molly Stark Byway), which offers a sweeping view south toward Massachusetts that becomes one of the most-visited fall foliage viewpoints in Southern Vermont. The overlook sits at approximately 2,400 feet and the view covers an estimated 100-mile radius on clear days.

Notes for writer: Hogback Mountain had a ski area that operated for decades and closed in 1986 — the old ski trails are still visible on the hillside in winter and early spring. The Southern Vermont Natural History Museum was formerly located here (at the old ski lodge building) — TODO: verify current museum status. The overlook itself is the main draw, but the Route 9 corridor between Brattleboro and Wilmington has additional stops worth noting.

# Photography Tips

- **Best angle:** The main south-facing overlook. Wide-angle lens recommended for the full valley panorama.
- **Light window:** Mid-morning light from the east illuminates the valley in fall. Avoid harsh midday sun.
- **Seasonal notes:**
  - *Fall (peak: first two weeks of October):* The primary reason to visit. The view covers multiple ridgelines turning simultaneously.
