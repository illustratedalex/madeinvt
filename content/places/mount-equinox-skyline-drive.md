Mount Equinox Skyline Drive is a privately operated toll road that climbs to the summit of Mount Equinox (3,848 ft) — one of the highest paved summits in Vermont accessible by car. The drive offers panoramic views across the Battenkill Valley, the Green Mountains, and on clear days, into New York, New Hampshire, and Massachusetts.

Notes for writer: The road is owned and operated by the Carthusian monks of the Charterhouse of the Transfiguration, which adds an unusual character to the destination. The summit has a small inn (The Inn at Equinox — TODO: verify current status) and observation areas. The drive itself is the experience — 5.2 miles of switchbacks through forest to open summit. # Photography Tips

- **Best angle:** The main overlook on the west side of the summit for Battenkill Valley views. South-facing ledge for Green Mountain ridge shots.
- **Light window:** Late afternoon on clear days for warm light across the valley.
- **Seasonal notes:**
  - *Fall:* Best season. The valley below turns orange and red while the summit may still be green — look for the color gradient.
  - *Summer:* Excellent visibility on low-humidity days. Bring layers — summit is significantly cooler than Manchester.
