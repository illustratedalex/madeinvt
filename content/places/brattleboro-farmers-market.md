# Visitor Tips

- **Parking:** Large lot at the main Saturday market location on Route 9. Free. Fills by mid-morning in summer.
- **Restrooms:** Portable facilities on-site.
- **Dogs:** Dog-friendly. Leash required.
- **Difficulty:** Flat, accessible. The lot and market layout are all at grade.
- **Visit length:** 45–90 minutes for a full walk and shopping. Plan for longer if you're eating a full breakfast from vendors.
- **Timing:** Arrive by 9 a.m. for best selection. Some vendors sell out of specialty items (pastured eggs, certain cheeses) by 10:30 a.m.

# Best Time to Visit

**August:** Peak produce season — corn, tomatoes, summer squash, berries. The market at full vendor capacity.

**May–June:** Early season — asparagus, greens, local maple, rhubarb. Market waking up for the year.

**September–October:** Root vegetables, winter squash, apple varieties. Fall baked goods. Last few weeks before the season closes.
