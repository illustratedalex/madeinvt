# Visitor Tips

- **Parking:** Free street parking and municipal lots in the village center. Easy on weekdays; busier on Third Friday event nights.
- **Restrooms:** Available at the train station and in local businesses.
- **Dogs:** Dog-friendly downtown — most outdoor areas welcome leashed dogs.
- **Difficulty:** Flat village center, fully walkable. The riverfront access involves some uneven terrain.
- **Visit length:** 2–4 hours for a full downtown walk, lunch, and river access.

# Parking

Free municipal parking in the village center. Train station has dedicated parking. Street parking on Westminster Street and Square is free and usually available outside event nights.


# Accessibility

Village center is mostly flat and accessible. Historic buildings have variable access — check individual businesses. River access trail has uneven terrain.
