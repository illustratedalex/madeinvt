# Best Time to Visit

**Spring (late April–June):** Peak water flow. Most dramatic conditions. Trail is muddy — waterproof boots essential.

**Summer (July–August):** Lower water but reliable falls. Better trail conditions. Best for a challenging day hike.

**Fall:** Good trail conditions, fall color on the approach. Water levels moderate.

**Avoid:** Winter — trail is icy and dangerous. Early spring mud season makes the lower trail a slog.
